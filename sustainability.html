Conscious Heritage

Luxury Rooted in Honor for Earth & Artisan

We reject industrial overproduction in favor of slow, deliberate craftsmanship. Every piece in our atelier is hand-spun, naturally dyed, and crafted to last generations.

01

Botanical Dyes

Our silk and cashmere textiles are hand-dyed using organic indigo leaves, madder root, walnut husks, and marigold flowers—eliminating toxic synthetic runoffs completely.

02

Small-Batch Micro-Loops

We work exclusively on bespoke micro-runs. By producing only what is ordered, we achieve a near-zero raw material waste footprint across all workshop guilds.

03

Direct Artisan Living Wages

We eliminate intermediaries. 100% of our production funds go directly into master artisan co-operatives in Kashmir, Jaipur, and Bengal, funding education and craft preservation.

Traceable Raw Fibers From High Altitude Pastures

Our raw Pashmina cashmere is hand-combed from free-roaming Changthangi goats in the high-altitude Changthang plateau of Ladakh. No animals are harmed, and pastures are managed in rhythm with nomadic seasonal migration.
