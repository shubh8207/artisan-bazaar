📍 Bagru, Rajasthan
Botanical Dabu Block Printing

Meena Devi & The Guild

In the desert village of Bagru, Meena Devi leads a collective of master block printers practicing mud-resist Dabu dyeing. Teak wooden blocks carved over three generations press organic indigo and iron mud into pure handloom textiles.

"When you touch hand-printed fabric, you feel the sun, the river mud, and hours of patient human concentration."

📍 Mayurbhanj, Odisha
Natural Sabai Fibre Weaving

Santhal Women's Guild

In Mayurbhanj, indigenous women harvest wild Sabai grass, sun-drying and hand-braiding it into durable strands. The guild provides dignified financial independence to over 200 craftswomen while preserving sustainable natural weaving.

"Sabai grass connects our forests to homes across the globe. Each twist strengthens both the fiber and our families."

📍 Johari Bazaar, Jaipur
Sterling Filigree & Gem Setting

Master Silversmith Rajeshwar

In Jaipur's historic Johari Bazaar, Rajeshwar shapes recycled 925 sterling silver into delicate bezel settings for natural moonstones and deep crimson garnets. His atelier has served patrons for over 80 years.

"No two natural gemstones are identical. The silver frame must honor the unique spirit of the stone."
